# Staged independent validation

Updated 9 September 2026. This is a prospective protocol, not a completed external
validation or an external preregistration. Existing published outcomes are already
known; record that exposure when registering an eventual independent test.

## 1. Data integrity before fitting

The hardened intake and all-reactor Kozlowski import are implemented. Preserve
all 15 reactors and 7,575 source observations, including excluded measurements.
See [the intake audit](../results/intake/README.md) for the two K1 blank decreases
and conflicting control dose entries. Neither issue is author-resolved.
Before changing the correction, compare the published three-blank calculation
with a clearly labelled sensitivity analysis; never silently repair the source.

## 2. Comparable prediction tests

`biochar-ad demo` and `biochar-ad fit` now compare constant Gompertz, log-linear
dose plus temperature, and log-quadratic dose plus temperature on exactly the
same whole-batch folds. All use the same robust loss, starting values and shared
parameter bounds. Each fit sees training responses only. Report every fold and
the arithmetic mean of fold RMSEs, weighting each batch equally. MAE is secondary.
Training AICc is descriptive because cumulative observations are autocorrelated
and the fitting loss is robust rather than Gaussian maximum likelihood.

The CLI writes `held_out_model_comparison.csv` and labels the lowest mean RMSE
candidate. This ranking is exploratory model selection, not an untouched final
test. The synthetic demonstration is generated by the quadratic model itself;
its ranking supplies no independent support for that model.

At one training temperature, Q10 is fixed to 1 and is not counted as estimated.
A fold with a new test temperature and only one training temperature is rejected.
At least three batches are required by this implementation. Shared lag remains
an explicit limitation. A convergence failure stops the comparison rather than
dropping a difficult fold. Bounds inherited from the demonstration must be
assessed and frozen for any new physical regime before final testing.

## 3. Independent data in two stages

### Stage A: dose at a fixed digestion temperature

Acquire an independent study with a zero-dose substrate control and at least
three nonzero doses of the same material, intact reactor replicates, inoculum
blanks, raw methane trajectories, correction inputs and redistribution terms.
Retain gas reference conditions, VS denominator, substrate/inoculum identity,
working volume and all exclusions. Convert dose to g/L only with an explicit
measured conversion basis; a percent-TS value is not interchangeable with g/L.

Freeze input hashes, material scope, QC decisions, fit bounds, candidate models
and splits before evaluating raw outcomes. Keep material types separate unless
an explicitly specified descriptor model is being tested. Use within-study reactor
holdouts for reproducibility and whole-dose holdouts (all replicates at that dose
together) for dose generalization. `biochar-ad fit-stage-a` implements both splits
explicitly from the reactor-level intake contract and treats whole-dose RMSE as the
primary Stage A metric. A within-study refit is not held-out-study transfer; the command
records that no held-out-study transfer was performed.

For eventual transfer, fit and select on development studies, then evaluate an
untouched study with no parameter refitting. If zero-dose recalibration is
allowed, declare a separate calibrated-transfer experiment and exclude its
control from final scoring. Report all candidate errors and paired differences;
do not invent independent samples from time points or treatment means.

### Stage B: digestion temperature

After Stage A, seek replicated designs crossing doses with multiple digestion
temperatures. Pyrolysis temperature is a material property, not digestion
temperature. Evaluate withheld conditions/studies and parameter identifiability.
Passing Stage A cannot validate Q10, industrial operation or a DIET mechanism.
